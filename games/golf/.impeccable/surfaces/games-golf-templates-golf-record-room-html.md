# The Record Room (`/golf/stats`)

Scope: the Pay Sheet's season in figures, Operate mode (inspect and compare; depth for the member who wants it). Audience: the stats-curious member on a phone on a Monday after a week banks, the one who wants ammunition for the group chat, and the one deciding whom to spend next; signed in or not. Job: five things, each findable from the first line: who led and when (the season race), the season's five lines (superlatives), who is earning on tour (Form Guide), who the room has spent (the Burn List, with a search), and who is still on the board. No action; the page is for reading. Proof: banked tournaments only, the season's enrollees as the room, the usage table for every burn, the tour's own prize money for the golfers. Constraints: the season race is the room's one chart and it is opt-in (drawn finished and still; it moves only on "Play the season"); no sparklines, no trend arrows, no podium, no medals, no gold; superlatives are lines, not awards; no Teko column of money; every read season-scoped; no query per row; the two scripts are local enhancements and the page is whole without them; a screen reader gets the race as a table.

## Direction contract

THESIS: The Record Room is a book with five leaves, and its first line says where each one is. It refuses the stats hub everyone ships: a dashboard of award cards with trophies over a chart that animates itself on load.

OWN-WORLD: The Pay Sheet as pinned in `games/golf/DESIGN.md`: cool paper, graphite ink, pencil, one pen blue. Each leaf opens on a 2px ink rule with its label (Teko caps, pencil) as the heading; inside a leaf, rows part on hairlines. The race is drawn in the room's three materials and nothing else: your line in pen at 3px, the leader's in ink at 2px, the room in pencil at 1px; gridlines are the sheet's hairlines and the floor is one ink rule. Money is Newsreader tabular everywhere; ranks are Teko, as on the sheet. The Burn List reuses the pick page's row and its hatch, the room's one texture (the share of the room that still has him).

STORY: A member taps "Stats", reads how far the season has run, and either jumps from the contents line to the leaf they came for or reads straight down: the race as it stands, the standings under it, then the five lines, the Form Guide, the Burn List (typing a name to find one golfer), and who is still on the board. If they want the season again, they press "Play the season" and watch the lead change hands in the standings, or drag the slider to a week.

FIRST VIEWPORT: At 375 wide, under the sub-nav: the H1 "The Record Room"; one context line (the season, "12 of 32 events banked", the other season when there is one); the contents line, five links in the room's link style on two rows between hairlines; then the first leaf: the ink rule, "The season race" with the word Banked at right, one pencil sentence ("Banked money, week by week, through the …"), the key (You, the leader by name, the room) and the top of the plot. From 1100px the page is the sheet's two columns: the leaves in the 720px column and the contents list in the 300px margin, sticky beside them under its own ink rule.

FORM: A contents line and five leaves, position 6 of the seven structures considered (the race leads in one widened column; two ledgers, the members and the golfers; the race with its standings in the margin; the lines lead; the Burn List leads with the race at the foot; a contents line and five leaves; gone and left paired). Seed key 358b0f2c; dealt 4, 6, 1 with 4 leading; Brad locked 6. The six catalog challengers were declined (the world is pinned); kept from the rail concourse split-flap board: the rows are the live things and the columns never move (in the replay the standings change places and nothing else on the row shifts), and from the airport sign system: a leaf is named the same on its sign and on its door (the contents link and the leaf's heading are one string).

FINISH: unreviewed and undocumented is unfinished; this build ends with the finish review, the verdict, DESIGN.md, and every shipping raster carrying its provenance.

Signature interaction: "Play the season". The race stands finished until asked. On Play a dashed pencil playhead crosses the plot, the lines are revealed up to it, a dot rides your line and the leader's, the line under the plot names the week, and the standings below re-order in competition rank, each row sliding to its new place. The slider under the plot steps the same thing by hand, one stop per event. Motion grammar: one glide per event (520ms, ease-out quart) and a 220ms rest on each; rows slide .5s on cubic-bezier(.16, 1, .3, 1). Under reduced motion there is no Play, the slider still steps the season, and rows change places without sliding.

Decisions carried from planning: the page is public and season-aware (`?season=`, the selector drawn only when more than one season has tournaments). The scripts live in `static/js/golf/` and are tagged with `?v={{ asset_version }}` (Brad, 2026-10-06). Banked means `results_finalized`; the race draws every member of the room in the sheet's order and in competition rank. The race's words (axis money, months, the two names) are text set over a drawing that stretches, so they keep their size on a phone; the two names sit inside the plot over their lines' last points, in a paper halo. The legacy replay's up and down marks and its gold and green dots are gone (no trend arrows beside figures; the pen is yours, ink is the leader's). The Record Room carries no scorecard tiles; it links to your scorecard. Still on the Board is the Burn List's complement: the top earners with no usage row in the room. The Burn List opens on its first twelve rows with "Show all" when the script runs, and lists every row without it.

Review rulings (2026-10-06, finish review, both verdicts ship): the lower of the race's two names sits under its line so it never lands on the leader's; a single event is one point at the plot's right end; event names are set after "the" by `the_event` on the server and in the replay payload; every member at rank 1 is a leader ("tied for the lead" in the key, "N tied" on the chart, every rank-1 line in ink); the Form Guide says "missed cuts"; a season with no schedule says so; the lines' terms are pencil sub-lines, never a second Teko label under the leaf's own; the readout names the lead after each event during the replay.

Unresolved: none.
