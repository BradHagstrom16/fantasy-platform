Overview

How It Works

Build a roster of nine national teams across five tiers before the first whistle. Every match your teams win and every round they survive adds to your total. The longer the odds, the bigger the multiplier, so a Wildcard on a deep run outscores a Favorite cruising to the quarters.

Buy-in is ${{ entry_fee }}. Rosters lock the moment the first match kicks off; after that, the board is frozen.

{# ── Tiebreaker ── #}
Tiebreaker

Tiebreaker

Tied on total points? The win goes to whoever guessed closest to total USA goals scored in the tournament. Still level after that, and the tied players split the pool evenly.

{# ── Edge Cases ── #}
Edge cases

Edge Cases

  • Extra time or penalties: A knockout win counts the same whether it comes in 90 minutes, extra time, or on penalties. No bonus, no penalty for how it is won.
  • Group-stage draws: In a draw, both teams bank the draw points, each scaled by its own tier multiplier.
  • Best 3rd advancement: Eight of the twelve third-place teams advance. Those eight collect the Best 3rd bonus.
  • Multiplier application: Every base value is multiplied by the team's tier, so the same result is worth far more from a Wildcard than from a Favorite.
