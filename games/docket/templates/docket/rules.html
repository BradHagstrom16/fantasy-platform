The week

How many picks
{{ scoring_slots }} sides a week, exactly. Any mix of college and NFL, any mix of spreads and totals.
One side per market
You cannot take both sides of the same market. A spread pick and a total pick on the same game are two legal picks, not one.
The deadline
Saturday 11:00 AM CT. Picks must land before 11:00:00; 10:59:59 is on time. Individual cases lock earlier, at their own kickoff, so a Thursday game is committed Thursday while the rest of your sheet stays open.
If you file nothing
The deadline pass fills your sheet: Overs on the highest totals and favorites on the largest spreads, drawn from games kicking off after the deadline. It never overwrites a pick you made, and it never assigns a backup.
The season
{{ total_weeks }} weeks, from college Week 1 through NFL Week 18, bowls and playoff rounds included. Weeks turn over Tuesday at 6:00 AM CT.
Entry
${{ docket_entry_fee }}, one entry per member. How the pot is divided is the Commissioner's ruling.

The headliner

One pick a week is your headliner and scores double. You name it yourself on any side you hold. It cannot be moved onto a case that has already kicked off, which closes the obvious exploit: naming Saturday's winner on Saturday morning.

Name none and the deadline pass names one for you, judged across your final {{ scoring_slots }} sides: the largest favorite, else the highest Over, else the earliest kickoff.

Held in reserve

Pick {{ backup_slot }} sits dormant. It scores nothing unless one of your {{ scoring_slots }} cases is thrown out, in which case it substitutes into the cancelled slot whose game had the earliest scheduled kickoff. Any further cancelled slots settle as a mistrial.

If your reserve's own case is thrown out, or you never filed one, every cancelled slot settles as a mistrial. A substitute inherits the headliner if the slot it fills carried it.

The ledger

Three keys, in order. Points decide it. Wins break a tie on points. The tiebreaker error breaks a tie on both, lowest first. Players level on all three share a rank.

Key 1, points
The sum of your weeks, less your worst one. The drop starts once a second week is graded.
Key 2, wins
Winning picks. A doubled win counts once here. Never dropped.
Key 3, the error account
Your tiebreaker error added up across the whole season, lowest first. Never dropped. The drop forgives points only, so a struck week keeps its wins and its error.

The number

One case a week is designated, and everyone predicts its combined final score to a tenth. Your error is the distance from the real total, and it accumulates all season.

Skip it and you are charged the designated case's posted total as your guess. That charge applies to every member every week, including weeks before you joined, so arriving late buys no advantage on this key.

Awkward cases

Overtime
Everything grades on the final score, overtime included: spreads, totals, and the tiebreaker number.
An NFL tie
Graded normally against the number. A tie still beats a spread you were getting points on. On a pick'em, where the line is zero, a tie is a mistrial.
A case thrown out
The Commissioner can rule a case No Contest, which is also what happens to a game postponed out of its week. Your reserve covers the first one; the rest settle as mistrials.
A bad line
A line imported wrong can be corrected before the deadline and before that game kicks off, with a reason recorded. Everyone holding that market is told to re-decide. After the deadline it is not corrected; it resolves as a case thrown out.
The designated case dies
Before the deadline, another is designated and the predictions reset to the new posted total. After the deadline, the week charges no error to anyone.

Where the lines come from

Lines are imported Tuesday morning and frozen. The first fetch that finds a posted market locks it; later runs only fill markets that were still empty. A locked line never moves again, and your pick is graded against the number shown when you made it.

Each market takes the first book on this list that has posted it, independently, so a game's spread and its total can come from different books. The book is recorded on the line and on your pick.
