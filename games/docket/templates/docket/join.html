A weekly pick sheet across college football and the NFL, from CFB Week 1 through NFL Week 18.

How the court works
Pick 8
Every week, pick 8 sides from college or pro. Spreads and totals are both fair game. A win is worth 1 point. A push is worth ½. A loss is worth 0.
Double your best
Choose one of your 8 as your Headliner. It scores double. Think you're right? Put a little more on it.
Break the tie
Each week, everyone predicts the final combined score of one designated game. Closest overall prediction wins the tiebreaker at season's end.
That's the game
Your worst week is dropped. The standings are decided by points, then wins, then the tiebreaker.

Picks lock Saturday at 11:00 AM CT, with individual games locking at kickoff. Once a game kicks off, everyone's pick on it is shown on All Sheets; the rest stay sealed until theirs. The finer points live in the rules.
