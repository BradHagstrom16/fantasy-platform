A weekly pick sheet across college football and the NFL, from CFB Week 1 through NFL Week 18.

How the court works
Eight sides a week
Any mix of college and pro: a spread side or an Over/Under, against lines frozen Tuesday morning. Win 1, mistrial (push) half, loss 0.
One headliner
Name your best pick each week. It scores double.
One in reserve
An optional ninth side that substitutes if one of your cases is thrown out.
The number
Predict the combined score of the week's designated game, to the tenth. Your season error is the last tiebreaker, and it never resets.
The season ledger
Points, then wins, then the number. Your single worst week is stricken from the record.
Deadlines
Sheets close Saturdays at 11:00 AM CT; each game locks at its own kickoff.
