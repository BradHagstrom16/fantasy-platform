## Problem Statement

A member enrolled in both games gets up to nine emails a week from two systems that do not know about each other: two "picks are open" letters 15 minutes apart on Tuesday, two reminders two hours apart on Friday, two more on Saturday, one on Sunday, a Survivor recap, and receipts. The Docket has no results email at all, so a member never gets "here is your record for the week."

Driver (Brad, 2026-09-21): "My own inbox, a docket record email is a good idea that I think adds value. I am willing to move around the reminder timings for both, the exactness of them is not really important to me... maybe I have dual games going on the site for future games where this could provide value too."

Counted on prod 2026-09-21 (read-only, `instance/overlap_count.py`, gitignored): CFB 32, Docket 19, Survivor-only 26, Docket-only 13, **dual 6, of whom 5 still alive**, dual with The Wire on 0. So 6 of 45 members are the merge's audience; the record letter reaches 19; the Paper's shape reaches everyone.

## What Makes This Cool

**The Tuesday Paper.** Docket grades at 05:15, Survivor opens at 06:00, the Docket opens at 06:15: three events in one hour with no urgency conflict. One club letter carries your Docket record, the week's top sheet and purse winner, who lost a Survivor life, how many are still alive, and "both boards are open." It is the one email a member opens by choice.

**The rescue nag stops being single-shot.** Today Survivor's final reminder has exactly one firing (T-1h ±35m catches only the 10:00 run; the pass exits at the deadline), so a mail outage at 10:00 means nobody is nagged. A wider window gives it an outage retry. The merged Saturday ends up more resilient than today's unmerged one.

## Constraints

- Live mid-season. Last season's final-hour nag "rescued real people."
- Games never import each other; `games/gameday.py` is the sanctioned cross-game shape.
- **There is no lock in code.** Reminder de-dup relies on systemd merging starts of the *same* oneshot unit (PR #169). Two units writing one flag can double-send.
- Sent flags are per game, per WEEK (`CfbWeek.last_reminder_type`, `DocketWeek.last_reminder_tier`), latched after the send loop on any success. `utils/reminders.tier_already_sent` is the gate.
- Reminder windows are symmetric `hours ± 35 min` (`should_send_reminder`, `active_window`). With an hourly timer that is exactly one firing per tier.
- Every member email is a `Letter` through `render_letter()` (ADR-058); `Letter.cta` is a single tuple.
- CFP weeks are hand-scheduled, not Saturdays. Nothing may key on a weekday in code.
- Three paths can announce "picks open" today: `cfb-spreads` (Tue/Fri 06:00), the ADR-062 retry in `cfb-scores` (Sun-Thu 08:00), and the Docket's `_run_import` from both `docket-setup` (Tue 06:15) and `docket-lines` (Tue-Fri 07:00).
- The game-day pass grades the Docket week Monday night and must send no mail (ADR-063, `tests/test_gameday_pass.py`).
- Both openers carry the Settle the Tab strip (ADR-056, test-locked), and the Docket has a second-bill strip invariant (`games/docket/DESIGN.md` §9).
- A new unit needs a preset rule (`tests/test_systemd_preset.py`); tests run on SQLite and Postgres 18; a new column needs its migration.

## Premises (as agreed, revised after the cold read and the spec review)

1. **The unit is the send slot, not the member path.** Each game answers "what does this member owe or what did they earn right now." One composer builds one Letter per member per slot. Survivor-only, Docket-only, eliminated, and already-picked members fall out naturally. No A/B/C branches in code.
2. **Every tier of every game is an anchor** (eng review 1A). Any game's active, unsent tier anchors a slot, keyed to its deadline and never to a weekday. If two games anchor in the same firing, the nearest deadline owns the subject. Another game rides only when it has an open obligation AND its own matching tier is near (see `covers`); riding pre-marks that tier. In a default week this yields three emails instead of five:
   - Slot F: Survivor warning anchors, **kept at T-25h** (T-24h would overlap the Docket 48h window by ten minutes). Docket rides and its 48h tier is covered.
   - Slot S: Survivor final anchors, **window `[T-2h35m, T-25m]`**. Docket rides as one line and its 24h tier is covered.
   - Slot D: Docket 2h anchors. Docket only.
   - In a week where Survivor has no matching anchor (no CFB week, or a CFP deadline days away), the Docket's 48h and 24h tiers anchor their own letters. Nothing is a special case.
3. **One Tuesday Paper, no hold** (revised). One Tuesday run opens both games and sends the Paper with whatever is open at that moment. Any game that is not open yet is announced later by its existing standalone path, unchanged.
4. **The Survivor recap stays on Sunday morning, untouched.** The Docket record is built standalone first so Docket-only members get it.
5. **Built on the `games/gameday.py` pattern.** Latching is per game: G's flag latches only if at least one letter containing a G section was delivered. Marks are monotonic (never regress a tier) and go in one commit. A riding game's failure degrades the letter to the anchor's section and never aborts the pass.
6. **This knowingly reverses the 2026-09-14 ruling** that the shared outbox waits for December. No two units ever own the same flag at the same time. Rollout state lives on the `ExecStart` line in the repo, never in `.env` (eng review 3A): `--anchor` names the games the desk may lead for, `--ride` names the slots that may carry a rider (5A). **End state: `club-remind` owns every reminder tier and `club-paper` owns Tuesday**, the old timers are disabled by name, and the legacy passes and units are deleted in that same step. While an old and a new unit coexist, `After=` edges (unit-file-tested) serialize them and the sent flags make the second a no-op.
7. A two-section letter uses `game_slug=None` (club chrome, gold CTA), which the shell already supports.
8. Out of scope: receipts, correction and redesignation notices, the per-member email-vs-push preference, wiring golf.

## Recommended Approach: C, built so Saturday is the last and safest step

### Two small contracts, not one big one

Each game exposes these from its own services package. The desk lists them the way `games/gameday.py::_consumers()` does, so adding a game is one consumer plus one line.

```
ReminderConsumer                      (reminder slots)
  slug
  anchor_tier(now)          -> this game's active, unsent tier right now, or None (every tier anchors)
  owes(now)                 -> {user_id: Section}    open obligations; None when out of
                                                     season or the week is not imported
  covers(anchor_slot, now)  -> the tier of THIS game a ride-along satisfies, only when that
                               tier's target is within 6h of now; else None
  tier_sent(tier)           -> bool                  wraps tier_already_sent
  mark_sent(tier)           -> stage the flag, monotonic, no commit

PaperConsumer                         (Tuesday Paper)
  slug
  open_week()               -> opens the week WITHOUT announcing; returns the open week or None
  headline(week)            -> str | None            club line, same for everyone
  section(user, week)       -> Section | None        personal lines + payment nudge
  mark_announced(week)      -> stage picks_open_notified (and record_notified for the Docket)
```

`Section` is content only: title, lines, link label, link URL, optional payment nudge. A new `section_block()` in `utils/email_layout.py` renders it with its own inline link. `Letter.cta` stays a single tuple: the anchor's link on a reminder, the lounge on the Paper.

### Reminder send rules

1. The anchor game computes its recipients through its shared `reminder_recipients()`, the same function the legacy pass calls.
2. Before composing, drop the rider entirely if `covers()` is None or `tier_sent(covers(...))` is true. This is what stops riders being re-mailed when the anchor retries.
3. Rider `owes()` runs in its own try/except. An exception logs at ERROR and sends anchor-only letters. `None` (out of season, week not imported) is a quiet skip, so the pass does not log errors hourly once the seasons diverge.
4. A dual recipient gets one letter: anchor section in full, rider as one line with its link. A rider-only recipient gets **that game's full standalone letter** from its shared `reminder_letter()`.
5. Per-game delivered counters. Latch G only if `delivered[G] > 0`. One commit.
6. Push is unchanged: each game pushes its own recipients with its own tag regardless of mail outcome.
7. Exit code: 1 if any anchor tier was active and delivered zero; 0 otherwise. `--scheduled` softens only out-of-season and week-not-imported, matching the Docket's existing rule.

### Windows

`REMINDER_WINDOWS` entries for Survivor gain explicit `start`/`end` offsets and a new reader replaces the symmetric check for them. A span of 120 minutes or more guarantees two hourly firings for any deadline minute, including an 18:30 CFP deadline.

- Final: `[T-2h35m, T-25m]` (default week: firings at 09:00 and 10:00 Saturday).
- Warning: `[T-26h35m, T-24h25m]` (default week: firings at 09:00 and 10:00 Friday). The end is pinned before T-23h35m on purpose: anything later re-enters the Docket 48h window (Fri 11:25-12:35), which is the overlap that kept Slot F at T-25h. `After=` orders the two units only when both are queued together; it does not protect a hand start. A test asserts the default-week Survivor and Docket windows are disjoint.

Plainly: the second firing sends only when the first delivered to nobody. It is an **outage retry**, not a second nag to people who still have not picked. In the normal case the final nag moves from one hour out to about two hours out for everyone, so every "one hour" string (subject, headline, lede, push body, the unit `Description=`, the CLAUDE.md command comment, `tests/test_cfb_reminders.py`) is re-derived from `format_time_remaining` in the same PR.

### One clock (eng review 7A)

The desk reads the clock once and passes `now` to every consumer call. No code reached from a desk run may read a clock itself. Survivor's new window reader takes `now` explicitly; the old `should_send_reminder` / `get_active_reminder_window` stay as thin wrappers that pass `get_current_time()`, so legacy callers and `tests/test_cfb_time_seam.py` are untouched. A test patches both games' clock readers to raise during a desk run with an explicit `now`. This is what makes `--dry-run --now <Saturday 09:00>` truthful in production, where the `*_FAKE_NOW` seams are off.

### Shared builders (eng review 6A)

Each game's reminders module exposes `reminder_recipients(week, tier, now)` and `reminder_letter(user, context, tier)`. The legacy pass and the new consumer both call them, so the rollback path is the live path's own code. Same for the openers: `run_spread_update(announce=True)` and the extracted Docket import service take an `announce` parameter defaulting to today's behavior.

### The desk letter shape (a second Club Letter shape; DESIGN.md gains it, 5A)

```

 eyebrow   The Morning Line · Week 4          (ink; club business)
 H1        Both boards are open               (state headline, see table)
 lede      one sentence
 ─────────────────────────────────────────── hairline
 eyebrow   CFB SURVIVOR · WEEK 4              (game accent)   <- sections in DEADLINE order
```

Rules: one section per game with something to say; one CTA per section and **no club-gold button when any game button is present**; sections in deadline order; `game_slug=None` chrome only when two or more sections render (a single-section Paper is that game's own letter: its eyebrow, accent, subject); subjects `The Morning Line, Week {n}: {state}` within 50 characters (the Paper's one relaxation of the 45-char rule); preheader carries the personal hook ("You went 6-2 and survived with Oregon").
